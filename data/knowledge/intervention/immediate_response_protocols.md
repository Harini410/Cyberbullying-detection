# Immediate Intervention Protocols for Victims of Cyberbullying

## 1. Golden Rules for Targets of Online Harassment
1. **Do NOT Retaliate**: Responding with aggression or insults escalates the cycle, provides perpetrators with ammunition to claim mutual harassment, and increases psychological burden.
2. **Disengage and Distance**: Step away from the screen immediately. Do not feed the trolls or validate inflammatory provocations.
3. **Preserve Objective Records**: Document screenshots, URLs, sender handles, and dates before any content can be deleted.
4. **Enforce Hard Digital Boundaries**: Utilize mute, block, and report features immediately across all contact surfaces.

## 2. Emotional Support & Mental Health Resources
- **Reach Out to Allies**: Confide in trusted mentors, educators, parents, counselors, or friends who can validate your experiences without victim-blaming.
- **Crisis Hotlines & Helplines**:
  - **988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline** (US & Canada): Call or text 988 (Available 24/7, free, confidential).
  - **Crisis Text Line**: Text HOME to 741741 to connect with a crisis counselor.
  - **The Trevor Project** (LGBTQ youth): Call 1-866-488-7386 or text START to 678-678.
  - **National Bullying Helpline** (UK): Call 0300 323 0169 or 0845 22 55 787.
  - **KIRAN Mental Health Helpline** (India): 1800-599-0019.

## 3. Workplace & Academic Reporting
- File written incident reports to human resources, school leadership, or campus safety.
- Request formal safety plans, altered seating/working arrangements, or digital communication restrictions where applicable.
