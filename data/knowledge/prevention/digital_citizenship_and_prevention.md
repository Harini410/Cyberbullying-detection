# Digital Citizenship, Proactive Prevention, and Bystander Intervention

## 1. Principles of Digital Citizenship
1. **Empathy-First Interaction**: Treat online interlocutors with dignity, recognizing the human impact of text communication.
2. **Critical Media Literacy**: Verify sensational rumors, malicious leaks, and digital manipulation before amplifying or forwarding.
3. **Respect for Privacy and Boundaries**: Never publish private conversations, media, or personal contact details without explicit consent.

## 2. Active Bystander Intervention: The 4Ds Framework
Witnesses have profound influence in halting online harassment dynamics:
1. **Direct**: Respectfully speak up to call out the abusive behavior directly (e.g., "This comment is unacceptable and harmful; stop.").
2. **Distract**: Shift community attention away from the cruelty by changing the subject, introducing positive discourse, or reporting the thread collectively.
3. **Delegate**: Alert platform moderators, community admins, teachers, or organizational leads with links and evidence.
4. **Delay**: Check in privately with the victim immediately afterward to offer empathy, validation, and practical support ("I saw what was said, it was wrong, and I am here for you.").

## 3. Institutional Prevention Frameworks
- Establish unambiguous, enforceable acceptable-use policies in educational and workplace environments.
- Provide recurring digital safety and emotional intelligence workshops.
- Cultivate peer mentorship groups and anonymous reporting reporting channels to lower barriers to seeking help.
