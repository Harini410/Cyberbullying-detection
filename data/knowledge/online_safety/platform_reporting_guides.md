# Online Platform Reporting and Evidence Preservation Guide

## 1. Digital Evidence Collection Protocol
Before deleting or blocking messages, preserving incontrovertible digital evidence is vital for institutional, legal, or platform escalation:
1. **Timestamped Screenshots**: Capture full screenshots showing the offending message, timestamp, sender handle/username, and surrounding conversation context.
2. **Profile & Post URLs**: Copy direct permalinks to the offending post and perpetrator's profile (e.g., https://platform.com/user/post_id).
3. **Internal Platform Identifiers**: On platforms like Discord, enable Developer Mode to copy User ID, Message ID, and Channel ID which persist even if usernames are changed.
4. **Offline Archival**: Export or store records in a secure offline folder or print them with metadata intact.

## 2. Platform-Specific Reporting Procedures
### Instagram & Threads
- **In-App Report**: Tap the three dots (...) on the post/story or message > Select 'Report' > Choose 'It's spam or it's inappropriate' > 'Bullying or harassment' > Specify if target is yourself or someone else.
- **Restrict & Block**: Restrict places comments from the user in approval mode without notifying them; Block terminates all direct interaction.

### X (formerly Twitter)
- **Reporting Steps**: Tap the three dots on the tweet > 'Report Tweet' > 'Hate speech' or 'Harassment'. Select applicable sub-categories (targeted slurs, intimidation).
- **Mute / Block**: Muting silences notifications; Blocking prevents profile viewing and replies.

### Discord
- **Trust & Safety Escalation**: Right-click the offending message > 'Report Message'.
- **Server Moderation**: Alert designated community moderators via private ticket systems before escalating to Discord Trust & Safety.

### TikTok
- **Video / Comment Reporting**: Long press the comment or tap 'Share' on video > 'Report' > 'Harassment or Bullying'.
- **Filter Keywords**: Utilize account privacy settings to automatically filter comments containing abusive keywords or phrases.

### YouTube
- **Comment / Video Flagging**: Click three dots next to comment > 'Report' > 'Harassment or bullying'.
- **User Block**: Navigate to channel About tab > Click Flag icon > 'Block user'.

## 3. Escalation Beyond Platforms
When threats cross the threshold into stalking, extortion, revenge imagery, or physical violence:
- Contact local law enforcement cybercrime divisions.
- Notify school, university, or corporate ombudsman or Title IX coordinators.
- Engage specialized cyber harassment support networks (e.g., Cyber Civil Rights Initiative, Cyberbullying Research Center).
