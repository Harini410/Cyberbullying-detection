# Cyberbullying Taxonomy, Indicators, and Behavioral Patterns

## 1. Definition and Classification
Cyberbullying is willful, repeated, or severe harm inflicted through computers, cell phones, and other electronic devices. Unlike offline peer conflict, digital harassment has perpetual persistence, rapid virality, and potential anonymity.

### Primary Forms of Cyberbullying
1. **Direct Harassment**: Repeated transmission of offensive, derogatory, or threatening messages directly to an individual via direct messages, mentions, or comment sections.
2. **Denigration and Defamation**: Dissemination of false or maliciously altered rumors, gossip, or digitally manipulated media to tarnish the target's reputation.
3. **Doxxing**: Malicious publication of private, identifiable information (e.g., home address, phone numbers, workplace, school, personal identification) to incite offline harassment.
4. **Exclusion and Ostracization**: Coordinated removal or systematic blocking of a target from peer communication groups, shared collaborative workspaces, or gaming lobbies.
5. **Flaming and Trolling**: Hostile, incendiary, and aggressive language intended to provoke emotional distress or trigger public online arguments.
6. **Impersonation (Masquerading)**: Creating fraudulent accounts or hijacking legitimate credentials to post inappropriate or offensive content under the victim's name.
7. **Cyberstalking**: Persistent, unwanted digital tracking, intrusive surveillance, and intimidation that creates a credible threat to personal safety.

## 2. Linguistic and Affective Indicators
- **Hostile Lexicon**: Explicit slurs, profanity, dehumanizing epithets, and death threats.
- **Implicit and Subversive Harassment**: Masked insults, dog whistles, sarcasm, coded acronyms (e.g., KYS), and coordinated passive-aggressive downvoting or ratioing.
- **Targeted Repetition**: Escalating cadence of unprovoked negative comments from single or coordinated accounts.
- **Affective Dimensions**: High concentrations of intense negative emotions, specifically concentrated anger, contempt, hostility, disgust, and terror-inducing intimidation.

## 3. Severity Criteria
- **Low**: Mild single-instance incivility, rude comments without targeted identity harassment or threat.
- **Medium**: Targeted insults, repeated teasing after objections, moderate emotional distress without explicit physical threats.
- **High**: Systematic harassment campaigns, severe derogatory attacks regarding protected attributes, impersonation, or encouragement of self-harm.
- **Critical**: Credible threats of physical violence, acute suicidal ideation encouragement, active doxxing, non-consensual imagery distribution, or stalking.
