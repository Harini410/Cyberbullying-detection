# Content Moderation Standards & Automated Enforcement Policy

## 1. Safety Policy Objectives
Online community safety requires transparent, consistent, and proportionate enforcement against abusive conduct, targeted intimidation, and harassment while respecting constructive discourse.

## 2. Threshold Classifications & Action Matrix
| Violation Category | Characteristics | Automated Action | Moderator Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tier 1: Severe Imminent Harm** | Threats of violence, suicide/self-harm incitement, doxxing personal addresses | Immediate quarantine & account lock | Rapid referral to law enforcement/crisis units |
| **Tier 2: Targeted Degradation** | Slurs against protected traits (race, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability) | Automated hidden visibility & shadow-filter | Human review for multi-day account suspension |
| **Tier 3: Persistent Hostility** | Repetitive insults, flame wars, harassment without slurs | Content flagged for context review | Warning issued; temporary mute on repeat offenses |
| **Tier 4: Ambiguous/Profane** | Casual colloquial slang, profanity without targeted malice | Allowed with optional sensitive content warning | No disciplinary action unless context proves harassment |

## 3. Contextual and Affective Rules
1. **Implicit Harassment vs Banter**: Automated systems must evaluate affective polarity (negative sentiment + concentrated anger/disgust) and user relationship signals.
2. **Reclaimed Vocabulary**: Contextual analysis must account for community subcultures and reclaimed phrases, preventing disproportionate censorship of marginalized groups.
3. **Appeals Process**: Users flagged for moderation must have access to automated and human appeal paths within 48 hours.
